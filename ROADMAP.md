## 📌 Phase 4: Master Priority Roadmap 🚀

1. **Textbook-Grounded Curriculum Ingestion**: Systematically ingest chapters and problem sets from the owner's canonical networking textbook into courses `NV-C01` through `NV-C05` with full source attribution.
2. **Assessment Pool Decoupling**: For high-stakes credentialing, decouple live exam question pools and scoring keys into a secure, private cloud evaluation service.
3. **Spanning Tree & Multi-Area OSPF Visualizers**: Complete interactive topology visualizers for RSTP and OSPF Area 0/NSSA border routers.
4. **Expanded Packet Forensics Scenarios**: Expand real-world packet capture traces for HTTP/2, TLS 1.3 handshakes, and BGP route flaps.
5. **Classroom & Cohort Management**: Cohort analytics, classroom progress monitoring, and instructor assignment dispatch.
6. **Advanced Infrastructure Labs**: Cloud VPC Peering, Kubernetes CNI networking, and IDS/IPS packet inspection labs.
