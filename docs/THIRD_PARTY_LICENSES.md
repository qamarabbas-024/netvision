## 3. Graphic Assets, Icons & 3D Shaders

- **Iconography**: Provided by the Lucide open-source project under the ISC License.
- **3D Assets & Topologies**: All 3D node meshes, router chassis, fiber optic cables, and packet particle effects are rendered procedurally using Three.js and custom GLSL WebGL shaders developed in-house. NetVision uses zero unlicensed commercial 3D models or stock photography.
- **Brand Imagery & Grid Textures**: Dynamic CSS SVG gradients and cybernetic grid patterns are mathematically generated without proprietary stock art.
